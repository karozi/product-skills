# Interview Guide (Mom-Test-Passing)

Target length: 30 minutes, 6 core sections.

## 1. Warm-up and life context (3 min)
- "Tell me about your role — what does a normal week look like?"
- "Where does [core activity] sit in that week?"

## 2. Last occurrence walkthrough (8 min)
- "When did you last [do the core activity]?"
- "Walk me through it, start to finish. What happened first?"
- "What was the most annoying part of that specific time?"
- Follow-ups: "Why that step?" "Then what did you do?"

## 3. Cost and current workaround (6 min)
- "What do you do when [the problem] happens today?"
- "How much time did the last one cost you?"
- "Has this workaround ever failed you? What happened?"

## 4. Prior attempts and spend (6 min)
- "Have you tried to fix this before? What did you try?"
- "Did you pay anything for any of those attempts?"
- "What made you stop using them?"

## 5. Priority check (4 min)
- "Where does this sit against everything else on your plate?"
- "What has this problem stopped you from doing?"
- "If it stayed exactly this bad for a year, what would happen?"

## 6. Commitment close (3 min)
- "Who else would care about this getting fixed?"
- "Could I see [the artifact: spreadsheet, ticket, email thread] you use for this?"
- "Would you be up for a 15-minute follow-up next week when I've got something to show?"
  (This one is allowed: it's a commitment ask, not a feedback ask.)

## Evidence bar
An interview counts as evidence only if it produced: a specific past event, a number, a spent
cost, or a scheduled next step. Otherwise it was a nice chat. Log which.
