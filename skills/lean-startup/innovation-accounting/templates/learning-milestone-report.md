## Reading rules

- A milestone that missed its threshold is a failed milestone. No partial credit.
- Flat cohorts after real tuning escalate to pivot-or-persevere — the report does not
  get to recommend "one more try."
- Every learning lands in a decision or a new milestone. Orphan learnings are deleted
  from the ledger's meaning, though the row stays for the record.
