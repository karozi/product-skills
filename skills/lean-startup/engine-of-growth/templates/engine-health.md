experiment. Fill every bracket.

## The one experiment

- **Intervention:** [one change aimed at the governing metric only]
- **Expected movement:** [from value] to [value] by [date]
- **Cost:** [spend / effort] · **Deadline:** [date]
- **Decision enabled:** [keep tuning / escalate to pivot-or-persevere]
- **Kill condition:** [result that ends this direction]

## Rules

- One experiment on one governing metric. Everything else waits.
- Non-governing metrics may be tracked for context but never drive decisions while
  the engine is unhealthy.
- A second flat result after honest tuning escalates — the report does not get to
  recommend a third try.
