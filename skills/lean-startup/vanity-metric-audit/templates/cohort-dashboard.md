## Reading rules

- Compare cohorts to cohorts, never to the blended average.
- A metric crossing its threshold is a scheduled pivot-or-persevere meeting, not a
  debate.
- Cumulative totals appear nowhere. If leadership needs one number, give the latest
  cohort plus the trend direction — never "total since launch."
