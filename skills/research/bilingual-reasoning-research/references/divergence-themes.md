# Divergence themes by topic type

Read during **Phase 4 (Divergence Register)** when the topic is not an AI model or a
developer tool. For AI / developer-tool topics, the themes listed inline in `SKILL.md`
apply directly; extend with anything below that is also relevant.

For every topic type, probe each theme by asking: what does Chinese coverage emphasise
that English coverage does not, and vice versa? Log the answer as a Divergence Register
row, tagged `[INFERRED]` or `[ORIGINAL]` if the divergence itself is not directly stated.

## Company / startup / M&amp;A

- Domestic-competitor framing: how does ZH coverage position this against local rivals?
- Funding-round framing: strategic backers vs pure financial backers.
- Regulatory posture: MIIT / CAC / TC260 filings or absence thereof.
- Talent-flow narrative: hires from BAT (Baidu, Alibaba, Tencent) or from Western labs.
- Localisation posture: does the company have a mainland entity, a HK entity, or neither?

## Protocol / open standard

- Adoption by domestic clouds (Alibaba, Tencent, Huawei, Baidu, Volcano Engine).
- Chinese working-group participation vs Western WG dominance.
- Fork or alternative-standard risk (e.g. domestic equivalents).
- Anti-sanction utility: is the protocol read in ZH sources as a way around US export controls?

## Research paper / preprint

- ZH replications, citations in Chinese labs' subsequent papers.
- Domestic-team co-author presence.
- Chinese-language explainer coverage on Jiqizhixin, QbitAI, Zhihu — often faster and more
  technical than English trade press.
- Framing as "catch-up" vs "leapfrog" in ZH commentary.

## Product launch (non-AI)

- CNY vs USD pricing (or absence of a CNY SKU).
- Availability on domestic app stores (Apple App Store CN, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi, OPPO).
- Payment-rail integration (WeChat Pay, Alipay) vs credit-card-only.
- Compliance posture: real-name verification, data-residency, algorithmic-filing (算法备案).

## Regulatory / policy event

- Which ministry or bureau is driving it (MIIT, CAC, NDRC, SAMR, TC260, MOST).
- Domestic industry pushback vs endorsement in ZH forums.
- Cross-border data-transfer implications.
- Comparison to EU (GDPR, AI Act) and US framings in ZH commentary.

## Controversy / incident

- Timeline discrepancy: when did ZH sources first cover it vs EN sources?
- Framing gap: harm to users vs harm to industry vs geopolitical framing.
- Silence patterns: which usually-vocal outlets did not cover it, and what does that
  suggest about sensitivity.

## Coined term / concept

- Does the ZH corpus have an equivalent coined term, and is it older or newer?
- Translation choice: literal (直译) vs functional (意译) vs untranslated (English kept).
- Adoption trajectory: academic → industry → consumer, or the reverse.

## Divergence self-check

Before finalising the Divergence Register, ask:
- Is at least one theme a *substantive* framing gap, not just a coverage-volume gap?
- Is the "why they differ" column tagged and defensible?
- Would a reader who only speaks English learn something they could not learn from
  English coverage alone? If no, the register is not yet doing its job.
